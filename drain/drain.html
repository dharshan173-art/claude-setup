RCC Open Drain Construction – Step by Step

1

Survey & Setting Out

Mark alignment with pegs & string; fix invert levels and slope (≈1:300–1:500).

2

Excavation

Dig trench to width + working space and depth to required gradient. Dispose spoil away from edge.

3

Compaction & PCC Bed

Ram/compact the bed, then lay PCC 1:4:8 (or M10), ~100–150 mm thick, to slope.

4

Reinforcement

Place steel bars for base slab and walls (e.g. 8–10 mm @150 c/c) with cover blocks.

5

Formwork / Shuttering

Fix steel or ply shutters for inner & outer wall faces; check alignment, plumb and oil the faces.

6

Concreting

Pour M20 concrete in base then walls; vibrate to remove voids; keep inside face smooth.

7

Curing & De-shuttering

Remove forms after 24–48 h; cure with water / wet gunny bags for min. 7–14 days.

8

Backfill & Finish

Backfill sides in layers & compact, plaster/finish edges, clean, then test water flow to outlet.
